# Privacy Policy — Morphix Insights

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Morphix Insights is a personal analytics tool run by Morphix for its own social media accounts: the
**Quran Vibe** YouTube channel and the **طمأنينة** Facebook Page. It runs only on the owner's own computer.
It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and nothing is sold or advertised.

## 1. Use of YouTube API Services

Morphix Insights uses **YouTube API Services** (the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube
Reporting API) to read statistics about the owner's YouTube channel. By connecting a YouTube channel you
agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

Google's handling of your data is described in the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 2. What we access and store

When an account is connected, Morphix Insights reads and stores the following, with read-only access.

**From YouTube** (scopes `youtube.readonly` and `yt-analytics.readonly`):
- channel name, id and subscriber count;
- for each public video: title, description, tags, thumbnail, duration and publish time;
- daily statistics: views, engaged views, watch time, average view duration and percentage, likes, comment
  counts, shares, subscribers gained and lost, impressions and click-through rate;
- the audience retention curve of each video.

**From Facebook** (permissions `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `read_insights`,
`business_management`):
- Page name, id and follower count;
- for each video and reel: description, thumbnail, duration and publish time;
- Page and video insights: views, plays, watch time, reactions, comments, shares, followers gained and the
  retention graph.

**Access tokens** issued by Google and Meta, so the tool can keep reading the data.

We do **not** read comment text, private messages, viewer identities, or any data about individual viewers.
All statistics are aggregate numbers supplied by the platforms.

## 3. How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner how their own videos perform and to suggest what to post next:
comparing each video with the account's recent videos, finding good posting times, spotting where viewers
stop watching, and grouping the same video published on both platforms.

## 4. Where it is stored and who sees it

- Everything is stored in a database on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server we
  operate, and no one else has access to it.
- Computed scores and comparisons are never published or shared with anyone.
- The only other thing that reads the data is the owner's own AI assistant, **Claude Code** (by Anthropic),
  running on the same computer with read-only access. When the owner asks it a question, the figures needed
  for the answer are sent to Anthropic's Claude service, which processes them to produce the answer. See
  [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- We do not sell, rent or share the data with any other party, and do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Cookies and device storage

Morphix Insights sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The only cookie is the login session of the local
application on the owner's computer.

## 6. How long data is kept, and how to remove it

- **Disconnect** inside the tool revokes access at once and deletes all data read from that account.
- You can also revoke access to YouTube at any time on Google's
  [security settings page](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). The tool then
  deletes the data read from YouTube within 30 days.
- To revoke Facebook access, remove the **Morphix Insights** app in Meta Business settings → Integrations →
  Connected apps. Facebook data is deleted when the account is disconnected in the tool. You can also ask
  for deletion at the address below.
- The tool checks every 30 days that access to YouTube is still authorised and stops syncing if it isn't.
- Statistics of a YouTube video that has been deleted from the channel are removed 30 days later.
